Overview

This is a brief overview of the political values and their associated characteristics.

Equality

You tend to pay attention to the average people on the streets. Oftentimes, you feel like the government isn't doing as desired.

Person of interest: Karl Marx, Mao Zedong, Fidel Castro, Che Guevara, Joseph Stalin, Vladimir Lenin, Ho Chi Minh

Countries of interest: China, Cuba, North Korea, Vietnam, Laos, Venezuela, Soviet Union

Quotes of interest: "From each according to his ability, to each according to his needs." - Karl Marx

Color inspiration: Communist red

Market

You tend to favor a free market economy with minimal government intervention. You believe in the power of supply and demand to drive economic growth and innovation.

Person of interest: Adam Smith, Milton Friedman, Friedrich Hayek, John Maynard Keynes

Countries of interest: United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, Germany, France, Japan

Quotes of interest: "The invisible hand guides the market." - Adam Smith

Color inspiration: Coca-Cola

Authoritarian

You tend to favor a strong, centralized government with significant controls. You believe that democracy and individual freedoms are less significant than order and stability.

Person of interest: Qin Shi Huang, Julius Caesar, Napoleon Bonaparte, Adolf Hitler, Joseph Stalin

Countries of interest: North Korea, China, Russia, Venezuela

Quotes of interest: “Who controls the past controls the future. Who controls the present controls the past.” ― George Orwell, 1984

Color inspiration: Iron

Libertarian

You tend to favor a government oriented towards individual liberty and minimal state interventions. You also believe that a government whom oppresses its citizens is inherently flawed.

Person of interest: Linus Torvalds, John Locke, Thomas Jefferson, Ron Paul, Ayn Rand

Countries of interest: Switzerland, New Zealand, Estonia, Iceland

Quotes of interest: "That government is best which governs least." - Henry David Thoreau

Color inspiration: Sky (As a symbol of freedom)

Internationalist

You tend to favor a global perspective and believe that international cooperation and diplomacy are essential for addressing global challenges.

Person of interest: Nelson Mandela, Kofi Annan, Ban Ki-moon

Countries (Entity) of interest: United Nations, European Union, African Union

Quotes of interest: "We are all inhabitants of one world." - Kofi Annan

Color inspiration: United Nations

Nationalist

You tend to prioritize the interests of your own nation and believe that national sovereignty and independence are crucial for the well-being of your people.

Person of interest: Adolf Hitler, Benito Mussolinl, Mustafa Kemal Atatürk, Winston Churchill

Countries of interest: United States, China, Russia, India, Japan

Quotes of interest: "My country, right or wrong; if right, to be kept right; and if wrong, to be set right." - Carl Schurz

Color inspiration: White (As a symbol of the KMT)
