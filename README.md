# 3D politics

This is a political testing application that is used for entertainment and educational purposes. Although there may be explicit characters such as Hitler, the ultimate goal is to understand what your thoughts are and how close [Ideologically](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ideology).

Note: this will include some political bias, despite my tries to maintain a neutral political perspective towards different ideology.
